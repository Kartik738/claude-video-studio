name: tv-color-grade
description: Controlled cinematic colour grading with Lumetri that protects skin tones. Use for grading, colour correction, matching shots, or cinematic looks.
---
Grading preferences:
- Order: correct each clip first (exposure, white balance, highlights/shadows), then match shots, then one look on an adjustment layer named "Grade: <look>".
- Looks (Lumetri starting points; apply at 60-80% strength):
  - natural: contrast +8, highlights -12, shadows +8, vibrance +10
  - warm-cinematic: exposure -0.1, contrast +14, highlights -20, temperature +8, faded film 6, vignette -1.2
  - mystic-night: exposure -0.2, contrast +12, highlights -18, temperature -9, tint +5, saturation 90, faded film 8, vignette -1.8
  - soft-luxe: contrast -4, highlights -25, shadows +12, blacks +6, faded film 12
- Protect skin: never let skin go green, grey or magenta. Prefer vibrance over saturation and keep global saturation at or below 110%.
- Don't crush blacks or clip highlights. Sharpen at most 15 in Lumetri.
