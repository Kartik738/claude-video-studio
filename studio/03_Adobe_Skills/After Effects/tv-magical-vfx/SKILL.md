---
name: tv-magical-vfx
description: Restrained, believable magical light effects using native effects (glow, CC Particle World, Fractal Noise mist, CC Light Sweep, CC Star Burst). Use for mystical, dreamy, tarot, fantasy or 'add magic' requests.
---
Preferences for magical effects:
- Magic should feel like light inside the scene, not stickers. Use at most 2 effects per shot and one hero effect per moment.
- Palette: gold FFD68C, moonlight D8E4FF, rose FFC6D4, ember FFB27A, violet C9B6FF.
- Build effects on their own named layers starting with "FX " (e.g. "FX Light Motes"), using Add or Screen blending, with fade in/out.
- Aura: an adjustment layer with a heavily feathered elliptical mask and Glow (radius about 80, intensity 0.6-0.9), gently pulsing.
- Motes: CC Particle World with a low birth rate, slow upward drift, small size, plus Glow.
- Mist: Fractal Noise (Dynamic), evolution animated by expression, Tint to the palette colour, screen blend at 25-40% opacity.
- Never blow out faces or the subject. Keep effects slow and elegant.
- Precompose and pre-render heavy particle layers on this 8 GB RAM machine if previews stall.
