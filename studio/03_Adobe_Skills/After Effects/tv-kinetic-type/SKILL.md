---
name: tv-kinetic-type
description: House rules for elegant, readable animated text and titles. Use whenever creating or animating text layers.
---
Preferences for all text animation:
- Max 5-7 words per line on vertical video. Hook text 80-110 px, body 54-70 px on a 1080x1920 comp.
- Keep text inside the safe zone (top 14%, bottom 20%, right 12%).
- Prefer per-character or per-word text animators (opacity 0, Y offset +40, blur 12) revealed by an eased Range Selector over 0.6-1.2 s. No bounce or elastic easing.
- Exit with a 0.3-0.5 s fade.
- Hold time: at least 0.3 s per word + 0.6 s.
- Add a soft shadow (distance 0, softness 40, opacity about 45%) or a subtle glow for legibility over footage.
- Use at most 2 font families. Elegant default: a serif display font for headlines and a clean sans for support text.
- Name layers "Title: <first words>". Check spelling before finishing.
